Collect helps Rwanda savings groups organize member records and review whole-RWF MoMo contribution activity.

On iOS, members can inspect group activity and use account and privacy controls. Private-group creation, the app’s MTN MoMo or Airtel Money USSD handoff, and consented receipt-SMS intake require an eligible Android build; these actions are not available in the iOS app.

A pending request or receipt SMS is not a confirmed contribution. Production confirmation requires independently reconciled settlement evidence or an explicitly approved constrained finality policy.

Collect never asks for a MoMo PIN or payment provider OTP. Your WhatsApp sign-in code is used only to sign in to Collect. Collect is a technology and record-keeping service, not a bank or wallet, and does not hold customer funds or provide loans or insurance.

For support, email info@ikanisa.com. In the app, request eligible data deletion through Settings → Security → Delete data. Collect is operated by IKANISA for Rwanda group-contribution workflows.
