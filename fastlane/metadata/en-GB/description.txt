Collect helps savings groups organize contributions, review member balances, and keep credit-readiness records in one mobile workspace.

Members create a contribution request, review the approved beneficiary, amount, and unique reference, then authorize a SEPA bank transfer in their banking app. Collect never asks for banking credentials and never initiates or signs a transfer. A contribution is recorded only after controlled bank evidence and the daily statement reconcile.

Groups can track collections, member participation, bank-transfer evidence, exceptions, and account status without relying on scattered chat messages or paper notes. The app is designed for savings groups and administrators that need a clearer record of who contributed, what changed, and what still needs follow-up.

Key capabilities:

- Savings group overview and member activity tracking
- Bank-transfer contribution and reconciliation review
- Credit-readiness records for organized group review
- Shareable group context for coordination
- Account and privacy controls for members

Collect is operated by IKANISA LTD for organized savings, contribution tracking, and credit-readiness recordkeeping. Collect is not a bank or wallet and does not provide credit or custody customer funds.
