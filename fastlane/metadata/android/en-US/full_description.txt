Collect helps Rwanda savings groups organize members, record whole-RWF contributions, and review reconciled group activity.

Members see the exact MTN MoMo or Airtel Money group receiver and amount before opening the provider USSD prompt. The member authorizes payment with the mobile network; Collect never asks for a MoMo PIN or OTP and does not hold member funds.

On eligible Android receiver devices, members may explicitly consent to Collect receiving newly arriving MoMo transaction-confirmation SMS for candidate reconciliation. Collect does not read SMS history, send SMS, or access Call Log data. SMS access can be turned off. An observed SMS is not independent settlement proof; pending and ambiguous contributions require governed reconciliation before a production confirmation claim.

Collect provides in-app account support and deletion controls. The service is operated by IKANISA for Rwanda group-contribution and record-keeping workflows.
