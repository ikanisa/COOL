Collect helps savings groups organize members, record contributions, review reconciled group activity, and prepare credit-readiness records.

Members can view the approved beneficiary, IBAN, BIC, exact Collect reference, and amount before opening Revolut or another banking app. Collect does not initiate, authorize, process, or custody a bank transfer. The user selects the beneficiary and authorizes the transfer entirely in their banking app.

On Android, a group receiver can optionally allow Collect to receive newly arriving MTN MoMo or Airtel Money transaction-confirmation SMS for candidate reconciliation. Collect does not read SMS history, send SMS, or access Call Log data. SMS access can be turned off at any time. A contribution becomes confirmed, changes a balance, and posts to the group ledger only after governed reconciliation.

Collect keeps account support and deletion controls accessible in the app and is operated by IKANISA for group savings, support, and credit-readiness workflows.
