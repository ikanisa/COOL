Collect helps Rwanda groups organize members and review whole-RWF MoMo contribution records. It is a technology and record-keeping service.

Private-group creation is available only on eligible Android builds, after SMS consent and device verification. iOS and web do not create private groups.

Eligible Android members check the exact MTN MoMo or Airtel Money receiver and whole-RWF amount, then approve payment in the mobile network’s USSD prompt. Collect never asks for a MoMo PIN or payment provider OTP and does not hold customer funds. Your WhatsApp sign-in code is only for signing in to Collect.

Eligible Android receiver devices may explicitly consent to newly arriving MoMo receipt SMS as candidate evidence. Collect does not read SMS history, send SMS or access Call Logs. SMS access can be turned off. A pending request or receipt SMS is not a confirmed contribution. Production confirmation requires independently reconciled settlement evidence or an explicitly approved constrained finality policy.

For a Collect question, email info@ikanisa.com. In the app, use Settings → Security → Delete data to request eligible data deletion. Collect does not provide loans, insurance or deposit custody.
