Collect helps savings groups organize members, track contributions, review group activity, and prepare credit-readiness records. Members can follow group rules, view contribution status, request support, and keep account and deletion choices accessible from the app. On Android, a group receiver can optionally allow Collect to receive only new MTN or Airtel mobile-money transaction SMS so contributions can be matched automatically. Collect does not read inbox history or unrelated messages. This access is explained before Android's permission prompt, can be denied, and can be turned off at any time from App permissions. Collect is operated by IKANISA for customers using savings, support, and credit-readiness workflows.
