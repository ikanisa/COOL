Collect helps Rwanda groups organize members and review whole-RWF MoMo contribution records. It is a technology and record-keeping service.

Any signed-in Collect user can create a private group and invite members without registering a MoMo number, provider or recipient. Group setup does not request SMS access or authorize payments.

Eligible Android members check the exact MTN MoMo or Airtel Money receiver and whole-RWF amount, then approve payment in the mobile network’s USSD prompt. Collect never asks for a MoMo PIN or payment provider OTP and does not hold customer funds. Your Collect sign-in code is only for signing in to Collect.

After a signed-in Android user enables SMS access, Collect automatically detects and selects newly arriving messages that appear to be MoMo receipts and attempts to send them as candidate evidence, without wallet registration or device pairing. Sending may be delayed or fail. No manual receipt upload or transaction-ID reporting is required. The filter can also send the full text of other messages, including sensitive information. The member app does not send SMS messages from your phone. It does not read SMS history or access Call Logs. SMS access can be turned off, but a message already being processed may still be retained. A pending request or receipt SMS is not a confirmed contribution. Production confirmation requires independently reconciled settlement evidence or an explicitly approved constrained finality policy.

For a Collect question, email info@ikanisa.com. In the app, use Settings → Security → Delete data to request eligible data deletion. Collect does not provide loans, insurance or deposit custody.
