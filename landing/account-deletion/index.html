Account Deletion

How to delete your Cool account.

Cool supports self-service account deletion from inside the mobile app.

Legal Center Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Delete inside the app

  1. Open the Cool app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt.

What happens next

When deletion succeeds, Cool removes the authenticated account and app-linked records that are configured to delete with that account in our backend. Some operational, fraud-prevention, audit, or legally required records may be retained for a limited period where necessary.

If you cannot access the app

Contact info@ikanisa.com from the email or phone-associated context you used with the account and include:

  • Your phone or WhatsApp number used in Cool.
  • Your request to delete the account.
  • Any details needed to help us identify the correct account.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
