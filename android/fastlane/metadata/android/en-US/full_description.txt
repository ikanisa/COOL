Cool is Rwanda's community finance and partner platform. Coordinate savings groups, send and verify Mobile Money payments, and access partner services from one app.

Community Groups
Create or join savings and contribution groups. Track member contributions, set payment schedules, and manage group finances with full transparency.

Mobile Money (MoMo)
Send money via MTN Rwanda MoMo USSD. Auto-verify incoming MoMo payment confirmations via SMS. View transaction statements and generate QR codes for easy payment.

Partner Services
Browse partner shops, access Rayon club merchandise, and discover local services.

Profile & Wallet
Manage your identity, wallet, and account settings. Track your activity and season progress.

Key Features:
• Savings groups with contribution tracking
• MoMo send money via USSD handoff
• Automatic MoMo SMS payment verification
• Partner shops, tickets, and club services
• Partner shop and club merchandise
• NFC and QR code payment tools
• Rise Season points and leaderboard
• WhatsApp-based authentication
• Dark-themed premium UI

Rwanda market only. English language.
