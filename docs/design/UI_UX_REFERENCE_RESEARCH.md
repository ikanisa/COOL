# Collect UI/UX Reference Research

## Reference Boundaries

Collect is not copying Revolut or Monzo assets, trademarks, fonts, colors, layouts, or screens. The references are used only to extract product-quality principles that can be translated into an original MoMo group collection experience.

## Revolut-Informed Lessons

- Precision and hierarchy: money views should place the most important amount first, then status, progress, and action.
- Scalable dashboard structure: the home screen should support summary cards, widgets, and fast navigation without feeling decorative.
- Data density with restraint: admin and ledger surfaces should expose status, confidence, amount, and review context in compact rows.
- Control through clarity: user-facing screens should make group privacy, Collect ID, receiver, and payment state obvious without extra choices.

Sources reviewed:
- Revolut 10 product update: https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_launches_revolut_10_as_it_targets_primary_accounts_and_passes_35m_customers_worldwide/

## Monzo-Informed Lessons

- Clear activity feed: a user should quickly see what changed, who contributed safely, and what needs action.
- Warm financial language: plain, inclusive, reader-first copy builds trust better than institutional language.
- Organize around groups: the product should feel like shared group money tracking, with pending intents, confirmed support, and recent activity.
- Safety copy belongs in-product: privacy and payment boundaries should be visible at the decision point.

Sources reviewed:
- Monzo Home redesign: https://monzo.com/us/blog/monzo-us-blog/new-home-screen
- Monzo writing principles: https://monzo.com/tone-of-voice

## Collect Translation

- Show RWF totals before secondary copy.
- Use calm precision for ledgers, payment status, and admin review.
- Use friendly but direct language for contributors and organizers.
- Repeat the product boundary: Collect does not move money; contributors pay directly through MOMO/USSD.
- Keep phone numbers, MOMO numbers, raw SMS, and private identities away from public surfaces.
