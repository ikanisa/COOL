What Happens Next
Heading
What happens next
Service Target
We aim to respond within 30 days or within the period required by applicable law.
We verify your identity.
We review pending transactions and group responsibilities.
We tell you if any action is required.
We delete or anonymise eligible data.
We retain only records required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes and regulatory compliance.
We confirm when the process is complete.
Before We Can Complete Deletion
Heading
You may need to complete these steps first
Withdraw or transfer any available balance through the relevant partner institution
Resolve pending contributions, reversals or disputes
Transfer a group-administrator role
Complete or cancel an active credit-readiness handoff
Download any statements you wish to keep
Address an active fraud, security or legal investigation
Data We May Retain
Heading
Data we may retain
Closing
Where a ledger entry affects other members, the transaction record may remain while your profile is deleted, restricted or de-identified as far as legally and technically possible. Deleted data may remain temporarily in secured backups until those backups are overwritten.
Financial transaction and audit records
KYC and AML records
Accounting and tax records
Fraud and security records
Records needed to resolve disputes or legal claims
Regulatory reports
Ledger entries needed to protect the rights of other group members
