## Reversal and correction lineage

Extend `financial_reversal_concurrency_uat.rb`, `financial_provider_ordering_uat.sql` and actual Admin reversal action callers once their existing request-ID operation binding is added. Test absent/untrusted/mismatched reversal evidence and withdrawn policy/authority: no compensating journal or balance change. In a valid synthetic mechanics case, real `admin_reverse_momo_payment` creates exactly one compensating journal with its `reverses_entry_id`, negative compatibility projections, accurate immutable reversal snapshot and append-only lineage referring to that actual journal. Exact current legacy reversal request replay remains a no-write acknowledgement; altered actor/payment/reason is denied.

A replacement is a fresh admitted receipt/payment, not a mutation of the original or a receipt resend. The replacement's source cannot be the consumed original receipt row. Add an append-only second lineage event linking the real original, reversal and replacement; do not update a null replacement ID in an immutable former event. Reverse/replace races and retry interruption must not duplicate financial effect. Confirm receipt content/currentness suppresses a reversed source and does not automatically catch up or resend from the correction lineage.
