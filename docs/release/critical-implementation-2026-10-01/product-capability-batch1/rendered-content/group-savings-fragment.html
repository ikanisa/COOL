How Collect works

01Create the group

Private-group creation is available only on eligible Android builds, after SMS consent and device verification. iOS and web do not create private groups.

02Invite members

Use a private link or QR code. Member operations require a complete Rwanda MoMo profile.

03Approve in USSD

Eligible Android members check the exact MTN MoMo or Airtel Money receiver and whole-RWF amount, then approve payment in the mobile network’s USSD prompt.

04Review the record

A pending request or receipt SMS is not a confirmed contribution. Production confirmation requires independently reconciled settlement evidence or an explicitly approved constrained finality policy.

05Future directions

Partner-led credit and insurance are not available through the current app. Contact IKANISA for information.

Features for groups that save together

Transparent group ledgerReconciled contributions are allocated to the authorized member and group.
Member recordsMembers review personal contribution activity within their permissions.
Group purposeGroups keep their own agreed contribution purpose and rules.
Role controlsAuthorized group roles see appropriate records; private details remain protected.
Purpose-based goalsA group can contribute toward school fees, assets or emergencies. Naming a purpose does not provide insurance, credit or an investment product.
Basic-phone plansA standalone basic-phone Collect journey is planned and is not currently available. The app’s Android USSD handoff is not a basic-phone Collect service.
No custodyCollect never asks for a MoMo PIN or payment provider OTP and does not hold customer funds. Your WhatsApp sign-in code is only for signing in to Collect.
Credit directionLoans, bank deposits, collateral arrangements and loan repayments are provider-dependent directions, not services available through the current Collect app. No lender arrangement, eligibility or approval is promised.

From rotation to accumulation - keep the trust, grow the capital.

Groups choose their own rotation or accumulation rules. Collect records contributions; it does not hold group capital, establish collateral, provide investments or promise credit. Any financial arrangement needs separate supported provider terms.

Each group chooses its rules. Collect does not force groups to abandon their existing culture or governance.
