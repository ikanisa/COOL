What each side brings

Current Collect role and planned scopes

  • Eligible Android private-group creation
  • Permission-controlled group and member records
  • Planned credit-readiness direction; no active service intake
  • Prospective micro-repayment direction; no active integration

A future licensed provider would need to supply

  • Regulated savings accounts and account operations
  • KYC, KYB and AML/CFT controls
  • Credit assessment and pricing
  • Loan disbursement

Potential provider value

A future supported provider arrangement could explore these outcomes; they are not observed Collect delivery:

  • New retail and MSME customers
  • Increased loan origination
  • Reduced pre-credit administration
