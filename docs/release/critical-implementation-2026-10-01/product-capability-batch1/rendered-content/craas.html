A planned approach to credit-readiness support.

Credit Readiness-as-a-Service is a planned service direction. No active Collect intake or coordinated preparation service is offered here. Contact IKANISA to ask about the plans.

Ask about credit-readiness plans Email Collect support

Payment access is widespread. Loan preparation support is not.

Small businesses often need finance but do not know exactly what a bank requires. Many lack structured records, cash-flow forecasts, collateral evidence or the corporate documentation needed to complete a strong loan file. Preparation services are fragmented.

01

What businesses face

  • Unclear lender and product requirements
  • Missing or expired documents
  • Weak business and cash-flow records
  • Expensive professional preparation services
  • Rejection before full credit analysis begins
02

What banks face

  • Incomplete files
  • Inconsistent applicant quality
  • Manual document checking
  • Delayed analyst review
  • High cost of small-business origination

Proposed preparation stages, not an active service

01

Loan inquiry

A future preparation service would begin with the business’s financing purpose and needs.

02

Service intake

An approved service would need a real intake, scope and responsible service owner. None is offered on this page.

03

Requirement mapping

Any future file would use a named lender’s current requirements, without promising eligibility.

04

Document preparation

Any professional assistance would require separately confirmed scope, availability and engagement.

05

Service coordination

Coordination and specialist services remain planned until supported delivery arrangements exist.

06

Provider review

A lender would retain its own assessment, pricing, approval and disbursement decisions.
