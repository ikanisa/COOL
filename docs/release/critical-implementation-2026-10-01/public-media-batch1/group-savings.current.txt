How Collect works
01
Create the group
Private-group creation is available only on eligible Android builds, after SMS consent and device verification. iOS and web do not create private groups.
02
Invite members
Use a private link or QR code. Member operations require a complete Rwanda MoMo profile.
03
Approve in USSD
Eligible Android members check the exact MTN MoMo or Airtel Money receiver and whole-RWF amount, then approve payment in the mobile network’s USSD prompt.
04
Review the record
A pending request or receipt SMS is not a confirmed contribution. Production confirmation requires independently reconciled settlement evidence or an explicitly approved constrained finality policy.
05
Future directions
Partner-led credit and insurance are not available through the current app. Contact IKANISA for information.
Features for groups that save together
Transparent group ledger
Reconciled contributions are allocated to the authorized member and group.
Member records
Members review personal contribution activity within their permissions.
Group purpose
Groups keep their own agreed contribution purpose and rules.
Role controls
Authorized group roles see appropriate records; private details remain protected.
Purpose-based goals
A group can contribute toward school fees, assets or emergencies. Naming a purpose does not provide insurance, credit or an investment product.
Basic-phone plans
A standalone basic-phone Collect journey is planned and is not currently available. The app’s Android USSD handoff is not a basic-phone Collect service.
No custody
Collect never asks for a MoMo PIN or payment provider OTP and does not hold customer funds. Your WhatsApp sign-in code is only for signing in to Collect.
Credit direction
Loans, bank deposits, collateral arrangements and loan repayments are provider-dependent directions, not services available through the current Collect app. No lender arrangement, eligibility or approval is promised.
From rotation to accumulation - keep the trust, grow the capital.
Groups choose their own rotation or accumulation rules. Collect records contributions; it does not hold group capital, establish collateral, provide investments or promise credit. Any financial arrangement needs separate supported provider terms.
Each group chooses its rules.
Collect does not force groups to abandon their existing culture or governance.
