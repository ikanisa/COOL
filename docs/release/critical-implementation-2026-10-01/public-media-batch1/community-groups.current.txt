What the current app supports
The Collect app’s USSD handoff and receipt-SMS access require Android. iOS and web support group records and account controls; they do not provide this handoff or SMS intake.
01
For group leaders
Create private groups on eligible Android builds
Invite members by private link or QR
Review contributions within authorized roles
Keep group and payer records separate
02
For members
Inspect the Rwanda MoMo receiver and whole-RWF amount
Use Android for the app’s USSD handoff
Review pending and reconciled contribution records
Keep personal evidence out of public links
Community use cases
01
Moto-taxi groups
Set a contribution purpose such as licensing, taxes or a future insurance purchase. Collect does not supply insurance.
02
Agricultural cooperatives
Track contributions toward inputs, equipment or working-capital goals without an automatic finance offer.
03
Women and youth groups
Keep clearer Rwanda contribution records. Credit-readiness assistance remains planned.
04
MSME associations
Coordinate group records for business goals. Loan and professional service availability requires separate arrangements.
