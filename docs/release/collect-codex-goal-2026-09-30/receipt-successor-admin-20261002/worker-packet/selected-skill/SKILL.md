## Fidelity and reporting

“100% Revolut” means fidelity to the selected visual typologies and colours, not use of Revolut's identity or business workflows. Own-app names, logos, route structure, task sequences, product content and actual capabilities are required adaptations, not defects to fix back to the source. A completion claim requires evidence for the declared scope: every applicable target route/state has a visual comparator or explicit adaptation, no unexplained material visual differences remain, and the target's own interactions/workflows are verified. Missing evidence is **unverified**, never an automatic pass. A percentage must state its numerator, denominator, exclusions and comparison method; do not convert a checklist score into pixel equivalence or require business-flow parity.
