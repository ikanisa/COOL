# Operations and recovery rehearsal preparation

This procedure binds the locally implemented Collect controls to seven incident drills. It is a draft operating artifact for C34/C35. No incident drill, accountable handover, owner assignment, live intervention or release approval is recorded. The request to stop asking for names is preserved; role assignments remain unresolved. The primary receipt policy and recipient/disclosure/language decisions remain pending in the separate receipt-policy-review packet.

Before a live drill, select one reviewed release manifest, exact deployed API and device builds, an isolated or explicitly allowlisted target and a protected evidence destination. Reconcile the installed RPC definitions, owner approvals, permissions, flags, active principals, queue state, native journal and scheduler inventory. The present local packets prove only their source-bound synthetic scopes. Hosted email is disabled at the latest public settings read; no current approved operator session or new source deployment is established.

For every drill, retain its original incident/action UUIDs, entity IDs, relevant revision/fence, checked/occurred/recorded times and redacted evidence links. Keep raw financial/SMS/contact material inside existing authorized evidence access. Record what was attempted, the observed durable outcome, every unresolved item, the independent reconciliation and a documented stop/resume decision. UI success, a heartbeat, an observed callback or an absent action record alone cannot close a financial or delivery outcome.

## Sender, carrier, network or device outage

Check Internet validation, SMS/phone-state permissions, selected subscription/SIM and telephony separately. Inspect receipt age, attempt state, per-part callback evidence and the exact native journal entry; none of those observations independently proves current activation or delivery. Keep the device's emergency pause available, preserve journals and resolve original request references. Avoid reinstalling, clearing application data, changing SIM or replacing keys during uncertain-send investigation. These can destroy the evidence needed to reconcile an already invoked attempt.

The organization-owned gateway currently has no approved run-bound dispatch authority or automatic loop. Its native interface starts paused and exposes no force-send/resume control. Any future automatic sender must pass the approved failure matrix before this outage drill can attest availability or backlog recovery. After repair, reconcile the original attempts first; unknown outcomes never authorize a new sender or blind retry.

## Uncertain cancellation, enrollment, revocation or send

Retain the original owner/project/purpose-bound checkpoint before any permitted mutation. After a timeout, lost response, session replacement or error following submission, keep the original UUID and expected intent. Fresh authentication may perform the supported original-actor lookup; in_progress, not_recorded, missing or expired lookup evidence does not authorize replay, clearing the checkpoint or making a replacement UUID. For cancellation, the backend permits only a pristine, canonical queued primary receipt with no attempts, claim, arm, handoff or confirmation. For a send, inspect the retained invocation marker and per-segment callback/journal evidence. Do not rewrite the primary payload, replace its lifetime payment/template identity, switch transport or infer delivery from a sent callback.

Keep receipt correction/resend pending until the explicit policy, linked identity, wording and permitted authority are implemented and approved. ## Compromised operator, supervisor identity or gateway

Use an independently current approved owner to revoke the affected email approval, supervisor principal or gateway identity through its narrow authorized API. The email revoke API cannot revoke the acting account and requires admin_users.manage; supervisor/gateway requests retain their original intent and UUID. Independently revoke compromised Auth sessions and rotate applicable credentials through supported platform controls with fresh readback. Do not use a recovered stale approval as current authority.

Retain original signing fingerprints, immutable requests, financial evidence and journals before controlled key replacement or device isolation. A revoked identity's historical reconciliation is not reactivation. Revocation or local pause cannot recall a carrier handoff already made. Prove the affected identity loses future authority and separately reconcile already issued attempts. Current source has no active automatic dispatch authority to claim has been stopped.

## Pause and controlled resume

Distinguish three controls: the native emergency pause preserves prior journals; the assisted receipt feature gate belongs to its existing authority; the future unattended operating-policy stop preference currently has no executable runner to control. Setting the future preference to Paused or clearing its stop cannot stop/resume the assisted sender, fixed signed shadow observations or handed-off carrier work. Do not show those preference changes as a global emergency stop.

New eligible paused work persists under the new backlog migration. Older missed work remains CATCHUP_POLICY_PENDING: lookback, historical/current contact/consent and receipt wording require separate approval. Before any actual resume, reconcile original uncertain attempts, review eligible backlog and contacts, verify exact current authority and approved caps/mode/sender, and retain an independent stop/resume decision. No live resume is implemented or authorized by this draft.

Keep the last known compatible source/artifact/API/configuration hashes and preserve current private action, audit, primary receipt and journal evidence. A client rollback requires compatibility with installed RPC/schema/auth behavior; avoid destructive migration reversal or re-enabling historical unsupported bank execution. Disable outbound integrations in the isolated recovery target before restore and keep them unavailable until source, authority and queues are independently reconciled.

Use an empty restore database, fail-on-error archive restore with ownership/ACLs preserved and a separately verified global-role/runtime bootstrap. Compare canonical rows, sequences, function/security definitions, owners/ACL/RLS and all financial/receipt/action projections before allowing reads. Restore actual Auth configuration, global credentials, Storage object bytes and sender journals through separately protected procedures: a database archive does not contain these complete assets. Independently reconcile post-snapshot revocations and invalidate obsolete sessions before reopening access.
