# Context sources and limits

Checked 6 September 2026. Sources inform context; their photos are not copied. Every scene is fictional. The selection is editorial, not a national prevalence survey.

## S1 — National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda

[Rwanda FinScope Survey 2024, savings discussion (page 44 in indexed extract)](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2024-09/Rwanda-Finscope-2024-Report_compressed.pdf)

Access: Search-index excerpt inspected; direct PDF fetch timed out. Not a full-document review.

Savings groups are an important informal saving channel; the indexed report describes about 52% of adults using savings groups. This is a 2024 survey figure, not a 2026 forecast.

Limits: No prevalence ranking is claimed for the 40 creative scenarios. No investment or savings product is authorised by this source.

## S2 — Rwanda Cultural Heritage Academy

[Ubukwe bwa Kinyarwanda — Gusaba no gukwa](https://www.rwandaheritage.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RCHA/Publications/Published_Books/Ubukwe_bwa_kinyarwanda.pdf)

Access: Search-index excerpt inspected; direct PDF access returned 403. No protected content retrieved.

The indexed historical account describes family preparation, representatives and gusaba/gukwa wedding context.

Limits: Historical cultural description, not a prescription for all contemporary Rwandan weddings. It does not substantiate every gift, committee or reception staging choice.

## S3 — Rwanda Cultural Heritage Academy via Google Arts & Culture

[The Evolution of Fashion in Rwanda](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/the-evolution-of-fashion-in-rwanda-rwanda-cultural-heritage-academy/0AXhd6Bj8G1ZHQ?hl=en)

Access: Full webpage inspected.

Umushanana is ceremonial attire, used at important events including weddings; the exhibit also describes contemporary Rwandan fashion.

Limits: Use for cultural direction; museum photographs are not runtime assets or generation inputs. Specific drum, garment and ceremony execution still needs local review.

## S4 — Rwanda Cooperative Agency

[About SACCOs](https://www.rca.gov.rw/cooperatives/about-saccos)

Access: Full webpage inspected.

The page distinguishes member-based savings and credit cooperatives from ordinary informal groups.

Limits: Background includes historical material. Do not portray Collect, an ikimina or every cooperative as a licensed SACCO or lender; no current partner or licence is established.

[Savings groups supported by the ECD Project empower parents to care for their children in HBECD](https://caritasrwanda.org/savings-groups-supported-by-the-ecd-project-empower-parents-to-care-for-their-children-in-hbecd/)
