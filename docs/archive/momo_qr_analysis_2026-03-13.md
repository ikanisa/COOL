# MoMo QR Deep Analysis

Date: 2026-03-13

## Previous defects

- The app emitted two incompatible MoMo QR payloads.
  - Profile QR used `momo://<e164>`.
  - The MoMo QR sheet used the raw phone number only.
- The in-app scanner only understood `momo://...`, so QR generated from the MoMo sheet could not round-trip reliably.
- The scanner could not launch a payment request automatically.
- The scanner was not tuned for QR-only capture, long-distance acquisition, or bright outdoor conditions.

## Current QR contract

- Recipient-only QR:
  - Encodes a COOL app link: `https://cool.app/momo?action=qr_profile&...`
  - Result when scanned:
    - COOL app opens the MoMo send flow with the recipient prefilled.
- Payment-request QR:
  - Encodes a direct dialer route: `tel:<encoded ussd>`
  - Result when scanned:
    - The dialer launches immediately with the country-specific MoMo USSD route prefilled.
  - Generation rule:
    - COOL only creates this QR after the user manually enters an amount and confirms generation.

## Important limitation

- A QR code cannot launch a correct MoMo USSD payment automatically without an amount.
- Because the country catalog templates require both `recipient` and `amount`, recipient-only QR codes must prefill the route rather than auto-dial it.
- This is why the receive QR and the pay-request QR are now deliberately separate states in the UI.

## Scanner tuning applied

- QR-only barcode format filtering.
- Android auto-zoom enabled.
- Higher camera resolution requested for better distant-code acquisition.
- Larger scan window for MoMo QR mode.
- Tap-to-focus enabled.
- High-contrast corner guides aligned with the real scan window.
- Guidance updated for glare and focus behavior.

## Compatibility kept

- The scanner still accepts:
  - legacy `momo://...` payloads
  - raw phone-number payloads generated by the old MoMo QR sheet
  - new COOL app-link payloads
  - direct `tel:` MoMo payment QR payloads
