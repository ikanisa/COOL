Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

Cool is a product of IKANISA Ltd, a company registered in Rwanda. We provide a community finance mobile application that enables group savings (ibimina), biometric payments (BioPay), and mobile money integration for Rwandan cooperatives and informal savings groups.

Contact: info@ikanisa.com · WhatsApp

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Savings Management: Recording and verifying group contributions via SMS-parsed evidence.
  • Payment Processing: Initiating USSD sessions and verifying Mobile Money transactions.
  • Compliance: Generating reports for BNR regulatory requirements.
  • Account Security: WhatsApp OTP verification and session management.
  • Product Improvement: Anonymized, aggregated analytics to improve features.

4. Biometric Data

Your biometric data never leaves your device. Cool's BioPay feature uses on-device facial recognition. No biometric templates, face maps, or raw biometric data are transmitted to, stored on, or processed by our servers. All biometric processing occurs exclusively on your phone using the device's secure enclave.

5. Mobile Money SMS Data

With your explicit permission, Cool reads incoming Mobile Money confirmation SMS messages to automatically verify and record savings contributions. This data is:

  • Parsed locally on your device
  • Stored as a cryptographic evidence record in our secure database
  • Used solely for ledger verification and contribution tracking
  • Never shared with third parties for marketing purposes

6. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

RecipientData SharedPurpose
Equity Bank / Urwego BankName, phone, contribution recordsCustodial account management
BNR (Central Bank)Aggregated, anonymized reportsRegulatory compliance
Supabase (Infrastructure)Encrypted application dataDatabase hosting with Row Level Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We do not use any third-party payment gateway APIs.

7. Data Security

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Every database query is scoped to your user identity.
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.
  • Evidence-Backed Ledger: Cryptographic hashing ensures transaction integrity.
  • No Payment APIs: Cool does not integrate with any payment gateway. Payments are initiated via USSD by you.

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for the duration of your membership.
  • Closed accounts: Personal data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request. Financial records required for regulatory compliance may be retained in anonymized form for up to 7 years.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time via the app
  • Correct inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete your account and data (see Account Deletion)
  • Export your savings history and contribution records
  • Withdraw consent for SMS reading at any time via device settings

10. Children's Privacy

Cool is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or SMS. Continued use of the app after notification constitutes acceptance.
