Account Deletion

How to delete your Cool account and your data

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion through any of the following methods:

1

In-App

Open Cool → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps.

2

Email

Send an email to info@ikanisa.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered phone number.

3

WhatsApp

Send a message to +250 795 588 248 requesting account deletion.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Data TypeActionTimeline
Profile InformationPermanently deletedWithin 30 days
Biometric DataAlready on-device only — deleted when you uninstall the appImmediate
Group MembershipsYou are removed from all groupsImmediate
Contribution HistoryYour name is anonymized but records are retained for group integrityWithin 30 days
SMS Evidence RecordsAnonymized and retained for regulatory complianceUp to 7 years
Bank AccountNot affected — managed directly by the partner bankN/A

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is irreversible. You cannot recover your account or data after deletion.
  • If you are a group administrator, you must transfer admin rights to another member before deleting your account, or the group will be archived.
  • Any pending withdrawals must be completed before account deletion can proceed.
  • Your bank account with Equity Bank or Urwego Bank is independent of Cool and will not be affected.
  • Regulatory-mandated financial records may be retained in anonymized form as required by Rwandan law.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 calendar days. You will receive confirmation via SMS once your account has been deleted.
